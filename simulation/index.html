Brunello 🐾

A shared diary for two pet parents. No chat. Tap what happened on one phone and the other phone hears it in the pet's own voice.

  1. Tap what happenedFood, poo, pee, a walk, a health note, a photo or an expense. No messages to type.
  2. The other parent is toldA notification arrives in the pet's voice, addressed to them by name.
  3. Everything stays on recordBoth phones share the diary, the photo album and what was spent, with who owes whom.
